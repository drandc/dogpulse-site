What DogPulse does

Daily health tracking

Energy, appetite, mood, water, bathroom, food and treats, exercise, notes — one screen, about thirty seconds. Tap the face that matches the day and you're done.

Every entry is dated and kept, so a vague worry in June can be checked against what you actually wrote down in March.

The difference

AI health insights

DogPulse reads your own logs and tells you what it sees: a treat that keeps showing up before soft stools, energy drifting down over three weeks, a weight trend worth a vet's opinion.

Each insight comes with the dates behind it and a confidence level, so you can judge it yourself — and bring it to your vet with something concrete to point at.

Insights refresh once a day.

Medications, vaccines and vet visits

Doses with reminders — tap once when given. Vaccines with due dates so a booster doesn't quietly slip past. Vet visits with the date, the outcome, the diagnosis and the prescription, plus documents you can attach.

Before an appointment, DogPulse helps you prepare what to ask.

And the Health Card: breed, age, weight, microchip, current medications, vet contact and insurance on one page — share it with a sitter or a new vet in one tap.

Weight, symptoms and the whole history

Weight over time as a chart. Symptoms logged as they happen. Photos gathered into a timeline you'll be glad you kept.

Then search the whole history by note, food or symptom, and filter by date or type — the way you'd want to the evening before a vet visit.

When a dog passes

DogPulse becomes a private memorial: their photos, their story, and gentle reminders on their birthday and the day they left.

Because they were family.

Questions people ask

What is DogPulse?

DogPulse is a dog health tracking app for Android. It logs your dog's energy, appetite, mood, water, bathroom, food, exercise and symptoms in under 30 seconds a day, keeps medications, vaccines and vet visits in one place, and uses AI to spot patterns across your logs.

Is DogPulse free?

Yes. Daily health logging, weight charts, the photo timeline, the food tracker and your full health history are free forever. Premium adds AI health insights, medication and vaccine reminders, vet visit PDF reports, support for up to 3 dogs and symptom pattern alerts. Premium is free for the first 7 days, then $3.99 per month or $27.99 per year.

Does DogPulse use AI?

Yes. DogPulse analyses your own logs and reports patterns it finds — a treat that keeps appearing before soft stools, energy drifting down over weeks, a weight trend worth asking about. Each insight lists the dates behind it and a confidence level. Insights refresh once a day. They are observations to bring to your vet, not a diagnosis.

Is my dog's data private?

Everything stays on your phone. There is no account to create, no cloud sync and no ads. Your dog's health record is stored on your device and shared only when you choose to share it — for example, sending the Health Card to a sitter or a vet.

What does DogPulse track?

Energy, appetite, mood, water intake, bathroom, food and treats, exercise, free-text notes, weight, symptoms, photos, medications and doses, vaccines with due dates, vet visits with outcomes and documents, and heat cycles. It also keeps a shareable Health Card and, if you ever need it, a private memorial.
