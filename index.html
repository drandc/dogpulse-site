What DogPulse does

Daily health tracking

Energy, appetite, mood, water, bathroom, food and exercise, notes, all on one screen, in about thirty seconds. Tap the face that matches the day and you're done.

Every entry is dated and kept, so a vague worry in June can be checked against what you actually wrote down in March.

The difference

AI health insights

DogPulse reads your own logs and tells you what it sees: a treat that keeps showing up before soft stools, energy drifting down over three weeks, a weight trend worth a vet's opinion.

Each insight comes with a confidence level, so you can judge it yourself and bring it to your vet with something concrete to point at.

Insights refresh once a day.

Medications, vaccines and vet visits

Doses with reminders. Tap once when given. Vaccines with due dates so a booster doesn't quietly slip past. Vet visits with the date, the reason, the outcome and the prescription, plus documents you can attach.

Weight, symptoms and the whole history

Weight over time as a chart. Symptoms logged as they happen. Photos gathered into a timeline you'll be glad you kept.

How it works

Four steps, about thirty seconds a day.

  1. 1. Log the day

    Tap the faces that match your dog's energy, appetite, mood and water intake. Add food, exercise, symptoms or a note if anything stood out.

  2. 2. Keep the record building

    Add weights as you take them, log medication doses when given, and record vaccines and vet visits as they happen. Every entry is dated and stored on your phone.

  3. 3. Let the patterns surface

    DogPulse reads your own logs and reports what it sees, such as a treat that keeps appearing before soft stools or energy drifting down over weeks. Each insight comes with a confidence level.

  4. 4. Bring it to your vet

    Review the history and generate a vet report, or share the Health Card. You arrive with dates and trends instead of trying to remember.

Questions people ask

What is DogPulse?

DogPulse is a dog health tracking app for Android. It logs your dog's energy, appetite, mood, water, bathroom, food, exercise and symptoms in under 30 seconds a day, keeps medications, vaccines and vet visits in one place, and uses AI to spot patterns across your logs.

Is DogPulse free?

Yes. Daily health logging, weight charts, the photo timeline, the food tracker and the last 90 days of health history are free forever, along with a first AI insight. Premium adds fuller AI health insights, medication and vaccine reminders, vet visit PDF reports, unlimited health history, food correlation insights and support for up to 3 dogs. Premium is free for the first 7 days, then $3.99 per month or $27.99 per year.

Does DogPulse use AI?

Yes. DogPulse analyses your own logs and reports patterns it finds, such as a treat that keeps appearing before soft stools, energy drifting down over weeks, or a weight trend worth asking about. Each insight comes with a confidence level so you can judge how much weight to give it. Insights refresh once a day. Everyone gets a first insight free; Premium unlocks the fuller analysis. They are observations to bring to your vet, not a diagnosis.

Is my dog's data private?

Your dog's health record is stored on your phone. There is no account to create, no cloud sync of your records, and no ads. It is shared only when you choose to share it, for example sending the Health Card to a sitter or a vet. Two things do leave the device: AI insights send the relevant log data for analysis, and DogPulse collects anonymous usage analytics to detect crashes and improve the app.

What does DogPulse track?

Energy, appetite, mood, water intake, bathroom, food and treats, exercise, free-text notes, weight, symptoms, photos, medications and doses, vaccines with due dates, vet visits with reasons, outcomes, prescriptions and documents, and heat cycles. It also keeps a shareable Health Card and, if you ever need it, a private memorial.

How do I track my dog's health day to day?

Log the same few things every day so changes stand out: energy, appetite, mood, water, bathroom and anything unusual. Consistency matters more than detail, because a single day tells you little while four weeks of entries show direction. DogPulse is built for this: one screen, about thirty seconds, and every entry dated and kept so you can compare months later.

Is DogPulse available on iPhone?

Not yet. DogPulse is currently an Android app, available on Google Play. There is no iOS version at this time.

Can I track more than one dog?

Yes. DogPulse Premium supports up to 3 dogs, each with their own logs, weight history, medications, vaccines and vet records. The free version tracks one dog.

Does DogPulse work offline?

Day-to-day tracking works offline. Logging, history, weights, medications and records are stored on your phone and need no connection. AI health insights require internet, because they are generated remotely. Anonymous usage analytics and subscription checks also sync when you are online.

Why use a dog health app instead of a notebook?

A notebook records, but it cannot compare. DogPulse charts weight over time, searches months of notes by food or symptom, reminds you before a vaccine is due, and reads your own logs for patterns you would have to spot yourself. At a vet appointment you can show dates and trends rather than trying to recall them.
